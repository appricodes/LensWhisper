# LensWhisper Privacy Policy

_Applies to LensWhisper (`com.appricodes.lens_whisper`). Same text as Settings → Legal → Privacy Policy in the app._

## Short Version

Your photos never leave your device. They are analysed by an AI model running on your own phone, are not uploaded anywhere, are not stored by us, and we never see them. We operate no server for this app and hold no account, profile or record about you.

Please note this does not mean the app makes no network connections at all — see "What does use the network" below.

## What Stays On Your Device

- Photos and video frames you capture or open. They are analysed on-device. A working copy is written to the app's private cache, which other apps cannot read; these copies are deleted automatically the next time the app starts (and earlier by the system if it needs the space). Nothing is uploaded, unless you choose to attach a photo to a report about an answer (see below).
- Your settings — language, description style, selected model, image size, volume shortcuts, and whether you have accepted the notice on first run.
- Any custom questions you record by voice.
- Notes you save about a scanned barcode — the text you dictate, and any voice recording you make — together with the barcode they belong to. These are held in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. They are also excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so they are never copied off the phone behind your back; the only way they leave is the export described below, which you start yourself.
- The AI model files you download, and the optional product database if you install it.

None of this is transmitted to us, except what you choose to send in a report (see below). Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Speech Recognition

When you use the microphone button to ask a question, Android's own speech recognition converts your speech to text. Depending on your device and its settings, that may be processed on-device or by your device manufacturer's or Google's servers under their own privacy terms. We receive neither the audio nor the text. If you prefer this never to happen, do not use the microphone feature.

## What Does Use The Network

- Downloading AI models. Model files are fetched from Hugging Face (Hugging Face, Inc., USA) when you choose to download one. Your IP address is necessarily visible to them as part of that request, under their own privacy policy. No other information about you is sent.

- Downloading the optional product database. If you choose to install it in Settings, a database of products is downloaded from our own server (wikiponder.com). This is an ordinary file download: nothing about you, your phone or anything you have scanned is sent. Once it is installed, scanning a product barcode is answered on your own phone, and no barcode is sent anywhere at all. The data itself comes from the Open Food Facts and Open Products Facts projects and is published by them under the Open Database Licence.

- Looking up a product barcode online. This only happens for a barcode the product database on your phone cannot answer — or for every product barcode, if you have not installed it. If you scan such a barcode and agree to it, the barcode number alone is sent to UPCitemdb (upcitemdb.com), a product database operated by a third party in the United States, to find out what the product is. Your IP address is necessarily visible to them as part of that request, under their own privacy policy. No photo, no location and nothing identifying you or your device is sent, and nothing is sent about a QR code — a QR code carries its own content, which the app simply reads. The app asks before the first such lookup and does nothing until you agree; if you decline, scanning still works without a product description. The information that comes back is supplied by that database and is not guaranteed to be correct.

- Reporting an answer. After an AI model has answered, a Report button lets you tell us about an answer that was offensive, harmful or wrong. Nothing is sent until you press Send in that dialog. A report contains the question the model was asked (including a question you spoke, as text), the model's name, its answer, the message you type, the app's version, and — only if you tick the box — the photo the model was shown. It is sent to our own server (wikiponder.com), which forwards it to us by email and does not keep a copy; that mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail), so the report is stored by Google, under its own privacy policy, until we delete it. Your IP address is necessarily visible to the server as part of that request; it is used only to limit how many reports can be sent in a short time and is not stored with the report. We use reports to find and fix problems with the app's answers, and delete them once they have been dealt with.

## Exporting Your Data

Settings offers an export of your barcode notes as a single file. It is written to this phone's own Downloads folder and nowhere else — the app cannot send it over the internet and cannot put it on any cloud service. Importing works the same way round, reading a file from Downloads.

Choosing to export still takes those notes out of the app's private storage and puts them in Downloads, a folder every app on the phone can see. The file is scrambled so that ordinary apps cannot simply open it, but it is not strongly protected: anyone with a copy of this app can unscramble it. Once exported, looking after that file — and deleting it when you no longer need it — is up to you, as is anywhere you afterwards choose to copy it yourself. We never receive it.

## Legal Bases (GDPR)

- Analysing your photos: not covered here at all, because it happens only on your device and involves no processing by us.
- Looking up a product barcode online: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by accepting the notice shown before the first lookup. You may withdraw it at any time by simply not using the barcode scanner, or avoid it entirely by installing the offline product database; withdrawal does not affect lookups already made.
- Downloading the product database: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by accepting the size and source shown before the download starts. It involves no personal data beyond the IP address any download necessarily reveals.
- Reporting an answer: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, given by pressing Send after the dialog has said what will be sent.

## Your Rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection under Art. 15–21 GDPR, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. In practice we hold no personal data about you, so a request will usually be answered by telling you exactly that. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.
